Upload & Rank

Add an SBOM and a vulnerability scan — what's running in your app, and what's wrong with it — plus an optional asset-context file that says which services matter most. Both files are read and scored right here on the page; with live threat intel on, only the matched CVE IDs are sent to FIRST's EPSS API.

Left box — your inventory. A CycloneDX JSON SBOM: every component your app depends on and how they depend on each other. Generate one with syft dir:. -o cyclonedx-json, or use the sample below.
Middle box — your findings. Scanner output: Trivy JSON, Nessus (.nessus/.xml) or a CSV with component,version,cve_id,cvss_base. It lists CVEs but says nothing about how much they matter to you.
Right box — business context (optional). asset-context.json marks services as tier 1/2/3 and internet-facing, and maps them to SBOM components or scanner hosts. Tier 1/2/3 multiply Impact by ×1.5/×1.25/×1.0, internet-facing by ×1.2; unmatched findings count as tier 2.
